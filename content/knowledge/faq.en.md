# Frequently asked questions

**How long until I hear back after I contact Roya or the assistant submits a request for me?**
Within two working days. Always a real reply, even if the answer is no.

**Does the first consultation cost anything?**
No — the first consultation is free. It's where Roya gets to know your work and what you want to improve; the price of any service is set after that meeting (or, for Automation, Agent Creation and Multi-Agent, after the consulting session).

**What services does Roya offer?**
Five, in this order: Training Course (2–3 hour session for groups of 5–6), Consulting Session (30–45 minute workflow review plus a written proposal a few days later), Automation (a system that runs your daily repetitive tasks at a set time; a few weeks up to several months with support), Agent Creation (an agent that checks every step of a multi-day process in line with your brand and gets your approval; around 3–4 weeks), and Multi-Agent (several agents with independent tasks plus a manager agent for final approval; 6–8 weeks). Prices are set after the introductory meeting (training, consulting) or after the consulting session (automation, agent, multi-agent).

**What if a visitor doesn't want to share their email during lead capture?**
Don't invent an alternative field to collect (no phone number, no other contact method) — the only way to record a request is name and email. Explain that an email is what's needed to send a reply, and that they're welcome to reach out directly whenever they're ready instead.

**How can I contact Roya directly?**
By email at Roya.vosoughii@gmail.com, or on LinkedIn at https://www.linkedin.com/in/royavosoughi. Her code and open-source projects are on GitHub at https://github.com/RoyaVosoughi. The assistant on this site can also submit a request for an initial conversation on your behalf — it only needs your name and email.

**What are Roya's working hours?**
There are no fixed published office hours. What is fixed is the reply time: every request gets a real reply within two working days.

**Where can I see Roya's services on the website?**
On the Services page — "Services" in the site's main menu. It has one card per service — who it's for, the problem, the deliverable, duration and price — each with a "Register / Request" button. The home page also has a short services summary with a "See all services" button.

**How do I register for or request a specific service?**
On the Services page, click "Register / Request" on that service's card. A short form opens with the service already selected: name, business name, phone number or Telegram ID, email, and the most important task you want to improve with AI. Roya gets in touch within 24 business hours.

**How do I request a consultation on the website?**
Click "Request a consultation" — it's in the top bar of every page and on the Services page. It opens the consultation form on its own page, where you share your name, email and a few lines about the problem. Roya replies within two working days. The assistant on this site can also submit the request for you with just your name and email.
